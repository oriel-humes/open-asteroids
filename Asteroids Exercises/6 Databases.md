# Databases

## Background

In the previous five exercises, you have used a variety of datasets to explore the properties of an real asteroid, deriving estimates for its orbit, absolute magnitude, diameter, albedo, rotation period, and taxonomic type. In this exercise, you will finally find out which asteroid you have been studying!

## Instructions

A number of services allow astronomers to access large collections of data about the properties of asteroids, including:

- [JPL’s Small Body Database Query](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/tools/sbdb_query.html)
- [Lowell Observatory’s astorbDB Query Tool](https://asteroid.lowell.edu/query-builder/)
- [IMCCE SSOnet’s Broad Flat Table](https://ssp.imcce.fr/webservices/ssodnet/api/ssobft/), accessible in Python using the [rocks package](https://rocks.readthedocs.io/en/latest/cli.html).

1. Using the database(s) of your choice, construct a query to identify asteroids with similar orbital and physical properties as your asteroid. Which asteroid(s) are the best matches to the properties you derived? Which asteroid do you think you have been working with this semester?

2. Record the parameters of the query and your guess as to the name of your asteroid. Explain your reasoning for this choice with reference to both the database values and the values you computed over the course of the semester.
